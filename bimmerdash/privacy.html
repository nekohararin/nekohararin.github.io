Privacy Policy for BimmerDash

Last Updated: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how BimmerDash ("the App"), created by an independent developer, handles information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information Collection and Use

Real-time vehicle telemetry, Bluetooth OBD-II data, GPS speed/location calculations, and on-device diagnostics are primarily processed locally on your device. If you choose to sign in with Apple, customize your profile, or enable synchronization and subscription features, we may collect and store limited account, profile, and device identifier information solely to operate, maintain, and restore those features. We do not sell your personal information.

2. Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party services to manage subscriptions and display advertisements:

  • RevenueCat (Subscription & In-App Purchase Management)
  • Unity LevelPlay / Unity Ads (Advertising Services)

These third parties may collect or access device and transaction identifiers (such as IDFA, where authorized) under their own privacy policies. All third-party partners receiving user data are required to provide the same or equal protection of user data as stated in this policy and required by App Store Guidelines.

3. Permissions, Tracking & Revoking Consent

The App may request Bluetooth, Location, Photo Library (when choosing an avatar), and App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permissions. You may revoke consent or adjust any permission at any time via iOS System Settings.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain data only as long as reasonably necessary to provide App features, using reasonable safeguards. You may delete your account in the App via Settings > Account & Sync > Delete Account, or contact us below to request data deletion.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to request data deletion, please contact us:

  • Email: bimmerdash.help@icloud.com
  • Support: https://github.com/nekohararin/BimmerDash/issues
