Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 14, 2026

Summary: WariMusubi does not require an account, does not display ads, does not include analytics or tracking SDKs, and does not collect or sell your personal data. All trip and expense records stay locally on your device.

1. Data We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to developer-operated servers. Under App Store privacy disclosures, WariMusubi operates as Data Not Collected.

2. Local On-Device Storage

All content you create in WariMusubi is stored exclusively on your iOS device:

  • Trip & Ledger Data (Apple SwiftData): Trip titles, companion names and avatar colors, expenses, notes, custom split allocations, settlement records, and saved exchange rate snapshots.
  • App Preferences (UserDefaults): Your chosen in-app display language and up to 6 recently selected currency codes for quick input.

3. Optional Network Requests & Third-Party Services

WariMusubi works offline for expense tracking, splitting, manual exchange rates, and settlement calculation.

An outbound HTTPS request occurs only when you explicitly tap Fetch Latest Rates inside the Exchange Rate sheet:

  • Service Provider: Rates By Exchange Rate API (https://open.er-api.com/v6/latest/{BASE_CURRENCY}).
  • Data Sent: Only the trip's base currency code (for example, TWD or JPY). Your trip names, companion names, expense amounts, and notes are never sent.
  • Server Logs: As with any standard HTTPS request, the third-party API provider receives standard connection metadata (such as your IP address) to deliver the exchange rate response. Please refer to ExchangeRate-API's own terms and privacy policy for details.

Disclaimer: Fetched reference exchange rates are for trip expense splitting estimates only; actual charges depend on your card issuer or currency exchange provider.

4. Sharing & Clipboard

When you tap Copy Report or Share, WariMusubi writes the formatted settlement summary to your device clipboard or passes it to the iOS share sheet for apps you choose. No data is shared automatically.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

You have full control over your data at all times. You may edit or delete trips, companions, expenses, or settled records directly inside the app, or remove all stored app data by uninstalling WariMusubi from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

WariMusubi does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any users.

7. Changes to This Policy

If future versions introduce features that change how data is handled (for example, optional iCloud synchronization), we will update this page and revise the effective date accordingly.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer at: bimmerdash.help@icloud.com
